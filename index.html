Order Up!

A daily cooking puzzle: put the steps in the right order and serve the dish.

Every day brings a new dish, with a single puzzle that anyone can start from their phone. Chef Cara, the carrot mascot, walks you through each recipe, and the bistro soundtrack keeps the pace light.

  • Mobile-first design, tested on a small iPhone
  • Hints cost points, so you choose when to use them
  • Order tickets show each dish's origin and calories
  • The archive of past puzzles is free for now

Tirada

A roulette roguelite: bet on the wheel, build your run, and beat the house.

Place your chips, spin, and try to survive the table. Every run is different: pick a character, collect chips and relics, and duel rival gamblers across three floors. The wheel grows as the stakes rise.

  • Three floors with a boss at the end of each
  • Versus mode lets you go head-to-head against a bot
  • Unlockable heroes, rivals and cosmetics
  • Online multiplayer is in progress

Old Maw

A daily dungeon crawler: one lantern, one relic, and one way down.

Descend into a dark maze with a single lantern. Everyone gets the same dungeon, hero and relic each day, so the whole world plays the same run. Grab the relic, then get back to the ladder before the dungeon collapses.

  • A new maze every day, the same for every player
  • Grab the relic, then outrun the collapse back to the ladder
  • One try, no accounts and no rerolls
  • A harder Gauntlet bonus dungeon every Sunday, with rarer relics
